PERSONAL RECORDING ARCHIVE

Voice Context

Voice Context is a private tool for organizing the owner's recordings and conversation notes. It imports audio from connected sources, creates transcripts and summaries, and backs up active audio and finished transcripts to private folders in the owner's Google Drive.

Personal Google Drive access is limited to files shared with the app or created by it. An optional, separate work Drive connection reads accessible meeting files; it cannot modify work Drive. Meeting media is excluded from personal Drive backups. The recording inbox itself is not available from this public information page.
