VOICE CONTEXT

Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026.

Voice Context is a personal tool operated for its owner. There is no public account registration or advertising.

Information used

The tool handles recordings, transcripts, call metadata, names and context notes supplied by its owner or obtained from sources the owner connects. This information can include private conversations.

Google Drive access

When the owner connects Google Drive, the tool requests access to files it creates or the owner explicitly shares with it. The optional work-account connection separately requests read-only access to work Drive files to discover shared Google Meet artifacts and read all transcript tabs. Meet audio or video is used for transcription when necessary but is not backed up to personal Drive. Only meeting transcript copies are delivered there. The personal connection uses its limited file access to back up active recording audio and available transcripts in the owner's private Drive folders. Each transcript is stored once; people and category tags appear in a recording index. The app does not make these files public. It stores a Google refresh token in private configuration so backups can continue while the app runs. The owner can revoke access in Google Account settings.

Other processing

Connected-source recordings are automatically sent to Soniox for transcription, and the resulting text is sent to OpenAI for summaries and categorization. Provider use is governed by each provider's own terms and privacy policy.

Storage and control

The app retains source audio and archive metadata in private local storage. The owner can move recordings to the app's recoverable Trash; app-owned Drive copies are then moved to Drive Trash. Completed Soniox processing copies are removed only after their results and originals are saved locally. Other providers apply their own retention policies. The owner can disconnect a source, revoke Google access, and manage files in their own Drive. Access to the private app and Drive folders is controlled by the owner.

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.
